About

Bridge research and field deployment for precision agriculture.

The about area supports team credibility, partner context, and a three-phase roadmap from water stress to broader crop intelligence.

Mission

Build an explainable, open-standard AgTech platform that helps researchers evaluate multimodal models and helps operators make safer irrigation decisions.

Phase 1 / Water Stress

Zone map, multimodal prediction, uncertainty and confirmation flow.

Phase 2 / Nutrient and Pest

Extend modality set and label taxonomy for nutrient deficit and pest risk.

Phase 3 / Scale

Multi-farm deployment, NGSI-LD integration, and API-ready decision support.
